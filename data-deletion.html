Data Deletion — EachLink Portal

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Operator: 3amiGlobal LLC ("3amiGlobal," "we")

You may request deletion of your connected account data from the EachLink Portal app at any time.

What we hold — and what we don't

The EachLink Portal app connects the "EachLink" Facebook Page and the @eachlinkportal Instagram account, both operated by 3amiGlobal LLC, to support content publishing, community engagement, and performance insights. Most information connected with EachLink Portal — your Facebook or Instagram profile, your comments and messages — lives with Meta, under Meta's own privacy policy and controls. We cannot delete data held by Meta; only Meta can, at your direction through its settings.

How to request deletion

Contact us from the email associated with your connected account:

  • Email 3amiglobal@3amiglobal.com
  • Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Tell us which Facebook Page or Instagram account the request covers

What gets deleted

Upon a verified request, we remove from our systems:

  • Authorized account tokens for the connected account
  • Stored profile data for the connected account
  • Analytics references tied to the connected account
  • Stored connection records for the connected account

Timing

Requests are processed within 30 days, after we confirm the request comes from the account owner or an authorized admin of the connected Page or account. Deletion covers data stored by 3amiGlobal LLC; it does not delete your Facebook or Instagram accounts or content held by Meta, which remain under your control in those platforms.

Deleting your information on Meta platforms

For information held by Meta, you can review, download, or delete it directly in your Facebook and Instagram settings — including your activity information and off-platform activity controls in the Accounts Center and your Facebook information settings. Deleting or deactivating your account there is handled entirely by Meta.
