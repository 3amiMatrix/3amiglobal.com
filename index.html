The Five Systems One connected ecosystem — five capabilities working together

Live Connection Hub

Secure, real-time video experiences: private rooms, scheduled networking, community discussions, creator interviews, and virtual events — with host controls, consent, and moderation built in.

Broadcasting Network

The Sphere meets audiences where they already are — YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and X programming and livestreams, gathered into one home and linked back to the original platforms.

Media Network

A central library of original productions, interviews, education, and community storytelling — Channel 3ami™, creative showcases, wellness programming, and Virtual Love Code™ productions.

Community Participation

Creators and community members contribute original videos and stories through a clear path: upload, safety screening, human moderation review, approval, publication.

Virtual Marketplace

Searchable profiles, category placement, direct links, featured campaigns, and subscriber dashboards — the engine that turns discovery into economic opportunity.
